Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

short_videos_4_big_team is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth the app accesses the developer's own YouTube account to upload videos, edit the metadata of uploaded videos, and read basic channel information (YouTube scopes: upload, readonly, force-ssl). It also reads files from the developer's own Google Drive folder.

Data use and sharing

This data is used solely to operate the upload workflow. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. OAuth tokens are stored privately on the developer's own server.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
